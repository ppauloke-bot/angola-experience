# Image Credits & Sources

This file exists so the client can decide, image by image, what can stay in a final/publishable version of the site. It covers **only** the real, licensed photography added in this round (`public/images/`). It does **not** cover the abstract gradient placeholders used everywhere else — those aren't real photos and have no source/license to track.

Per `Website-Strategy.md` §11.2 (tier 3), every image here is landscape/landmark photography — no identifiable person is the subject of any image. A few show small, distant, unposed figures incidental to a public place (e.g. fishing boats, a beach in the background); none are staged "tourist enjoying their trip" photography and none are presented as Angola Experience customers.

All images are from Wikimedia Commons, all under licenses that permit reuse with attribution. **Before any real/public use of this site, re-verify each license hasn't changed on Commons and add visible attribution wherever the license requires it** (CC BY / CC BY-SA licenses legally require attribution even though this demo doesn't yet display on-page credits — see "Known gap" at the bottom).

## Deliberately left as abstract placeholders (not oversights)

- **`ilha-do-mussulo`** experience — no verified photo of Mussulo specifically was found on Wikimedia Commons (only of "Ilha de Luanda," a different, adjacent peninsula).
- **`quedas-de-musseleje`** experience — no photo found distinct from Calandula.
- **`consultoria-roteiro-personalizado`** and **`servicos-de-concierge`** — these are advisory services, not places; real destination photography doesn't apply to them, so they intentionally keep the abstract treatment.
- **Homepage hero (`Hero.tsx`) and 404 page (`not-found.tsx`)** — both use a generic "Angola" caption not tied to any single province/experience data record, so there's no specific real place to source and no `heroImageSrc` field wiring them up.
- **Cabinda** — every candidate found was either a people-focused photo (a 2024 Wikimedia-community workshop outing, not a place) or a 2100×300 Wikivoyage banner crop too extreme an aspect ratio to reframe into a 16:9 hero without losing most of its resolution.
- **Uíge** — one candidate was found (`Rua Uige.JPG`, CC0), but a person carrying a load is a fairly prominent, central compositional element in it, not a small/incidental figure — rejected under the same "no identifiable person as the subject" rule applied everywhere else, rather than relaxed for this one province.
- **Zaire** (M'Banza Kongo) — the UNESCO-listed Kulumbimbi ruins have no photo currently on Commons; the only real photos found in the M'Banza Kongo category are healthcare-outreach and carnival-crowd photography, both people-focused and unrelated to the "real place, not real people" split.
- **Lunda Norte, Cuando, Cubango, Cunene, Moxico Leste** — no usable landscape/landmark photography found on Wikimedia Commons for these; several are among the newest provinces (created 2024) with very little photographic coverage yet. Left abstract rather than force a mismatched or low-quality result.

## Known gap

None of these images currently display an on-page, human-visible attribution credit (e.g. "Photo: Paulo César Santos, CC0, Wikimedia Commons"). CC0/public-domain images don't legally require one; the CC BY and CC BY-SA images technically do. This wasn't added in this round since it wasn't part of the requested scope — worth resolving before any real/public use of the site.
