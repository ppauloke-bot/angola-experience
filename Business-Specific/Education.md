# Purpose

This document defines specialized principles for designing and developing digital experiences for educational organizations.

Supported businesses include:

- Schools
- Universities
- Training Centers
- Online Education Platforms
- Language Schools
- Professional Courses
- Educational Institutions

---

# Core Principle

Education websites do not sell classes.

They sell growth and opportunity.

Students and families are choosing:

- knowledge;
- development;
- future opportunities;
- personal transformation.

The website should communicate the value of learning.

---

# Business Objectives

An education website should:

- attract students;
- generate applications;
- build institutional trust;
- communicate programs;
- strengthen reputation.

---

# Education Customer Psychology

Visitors usually ask:

- Is this institution trustworthy?
- Will this education create opportunities?
- Are the teachers qualified?
- Is this the right choice for me?

The website should reduce uncertainty.

↓

Programs Exploration

↓

Trust Building

↓

Application Decision

↓

Enrollment

# Recommended Website Structure

A professional education website usually includes:

1. Homepage
2. Programs / Courses
3. Admissions
4. Faculty / Teachers
5. Student Life
6. Facilities
7. Results & Achievements
8. News / Events
9. Testimonials
10. Contact

---

# Homepage Strategy

The homepage should immediately communicate:

- what the institution offers;
- who it serves;
- why it is different.

Important elements:

- strong positioning;
- key programs;
- success stories;
- application CTA.

---

# Program Pages

Every course or program should have a dedicated page.

Include:

- overview;
- objectives;
- curriculum;
- duration;
- requirements;
- schedule;
- career opportunities.

---

# Course Presentation

Avoid only listing subjects.

Explain:

- what students will learn;
- which skills they develop;
- what opportunities become available.

Education is about outcomes.

---

# Admissions Experience

The application process should be clear.

Explain:

- requirements;
- deadlines;
- documents;
- steps;
- fees;
- next actions.

---

# Application Conversion

Reduce friction.

Provide:

- online application;
- inquiry forms;
- campus visit requests;
- contact with admissions team.

---

# Student Journey

Consider the complete experience:

Before enrollment:

- research;
- comparison;
- questions.

During enrollment:

- application;
- documentation;
- communication.

After enrollment:

- community;
- support;
- engagement.

# Education Industry Anti-Patterns

Common mistakes include:

---

## Information Overload

Problem:

Visitors cannot understand the institution.

Solution:

Organize information clearly.

---

## Weak Program Pages

Problem:

Courses are only listed without explaining value.

Solution:

Show outcomes and opportunities.

---

## Lack of Student Proof

Problem:

Visitors cannot imagine the experience.

Solution:

Show student stories and results.

---

## Difficult Applications

Problem:

Students abandon the process.

Solution:

Simplify enrollment journeys.

---

## Outdated Information

Problem:

Incorrect dates or programs reduce trust.

Solution:

Maintain accurate content.
