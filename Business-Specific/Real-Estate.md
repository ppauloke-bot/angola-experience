# Real Estate

Version: 1.0

Industry: Real Estate & Property

---

# Purpose

This document defines specialized principles for designing and developing premium digital experiences for real estate companies, agents, developers and property platforms.

The goal is to create websites that communicate:

- trust;
- value;
- lifestyle;
- opportunity;
- confidence.

---

# Core Principle

A real estate website is not a property catalog.

It is a digital sales environment.

People do not only buy buildings.

They buy:

- locations;
- lifestyles;
- security;
- opportunities;
- future possibilities.

---

# How Real Estate Experts Think

A successful real estate website answers:

"Why should someone trust this company to help with one of the biggest decisions of their life?"

Every element should reinforce:

- professionalism;
- credibility;
- property value;
- market expertise.

---

# Homepage Strategy

The homepage should immediately answer:

- What kind of properties are available?
- Where?
- Why trust this company?

The homepage should encourage exploration immediately.

---

# Hero Search Experience

Unlike many industries, search can be the primary hero element.

Recommended search filters:

- Buy / Rent
- Property Type
- Location
- Bedrooms
- Price Range

Users should begin searching immediately.

---

# Property Search UX

Search should feel effortless.

Allow filtering by:

- location
- property type
- bedrooms
- bathrooms
- area
- price
- amenities
- availability
- new construction
- luxury
- furnished

The fewer clicks required, the better.

---

# Smart Search

Whenever possible support:

- predictive search
- autocomplete
- suggested locations
- recent searches

Reduce typing effort.

---

# Saved Searches

Allow users to save searches.

Benefits:

- encourages return visits
- improves long-term engagement
- supports lead nurturing

---

# Visual Storytelling Strategy

Every visual asset should have a purpose.

Photography informs.

Video inspires.

Drone footage provides context.

Floor plans explain.

---

# Photography Strategy

Property photography should communicate:

- space;
- light;
- quality;
- atmosphere.

Avoid:

- poor lighting;
- distorted perspectives;
- inconsistent editing.

---

# Hero Photography

The first image should immediately create desire.

Choose the strongest visual that best represents the property's identity.

---

# Interior Photography

Focus on:

- natural light;
- spaciousness;
- materials;
- textures;
- craftsmanship.

---

# Exterior Photography

Communicate:

- architecture;
- landscaping;
- surroundings;
- arrival experience.

---

# Lifestyle Photography

Show people naturally experiencing spaces.

Examples:

- family breakfast;
- relaxing terrace;
- evening lighting;
- workspace;
- outdoor living.

Lifestyle creates emotional connection.

---

# Cinematic Video Strategy

Video should communicate what photography cannot.

Good examples:

- walking through the property;
- changing natural light;
- atmosphere;
- movement between spaces.

Video should feel calm, premium and intentional.

---

# Drone Footage

Drone footage is valuable because it explains context.

Show:

- neighborhood;
- coastline;
- mountains;
- accessibility;
- nearby amenities;
- surrounding architecture.

Avoid random aerial footage.

Every shot should answer:

"Why is this location valuable?"

---

# Motion Design

Motion should guide attention.

Examples:

- elegant image transitions;
- progressive reveals;
- smooth scrolling;
- subtle parallax;
- cinematic section changes.

Avoid excessive animation.

Luxury is usually quiet.

---

# Progressive Media Strategy

Introduce media gradually.

Suggested order:

1. Hero photography
2. Interior gallery
3. Video
4. Drone footage
5. Floor plans
6. Virtual tour
7. Interactive map

Each step reduces uncertainty.

---

# Industry Anti-Patterns

Common mistakes include:

---

## Property Catalog Mentality

Problem:

The website only lists properties.

Solution:

Create guided discovery experiences.

---

## Weak Visual Presentation

Problem:

Photography fails to communicate value.

Solution:

Invest in premium visual storytelling.

---

## Poor Search Experience

Problem:

Finding properties requires excessive effort.

Solution:

Design intuitive search and filtering.

---

## Information Overload

Problem:

Visitors receive too much information too early.

Solution:

Reveal information progressively.

---

## Weak Mobile Experience

Problem:

Searching properties becomes frustrating.

Solution:

Prioritize mobile-first usability.

---

## Lack of Trust

Problem:

Visitors cannot evaluate credibility.

Solution:

Highlight expertise, transparency and social proof.
