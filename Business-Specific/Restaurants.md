# Restaurants

Version: 1.0

Industry: Food & Hospitality

---

# Purpose

This document defines specialized principles for designing and developing premium digital experiences for restaurants.

The goal is to create websites that transform visitors into customers by communicating:

- taste;
- atmosphere;
- identity;
- trust;
- experience.

---

# Core Principle

People do not choose restaurants only because of food.

They choose:

- experiences;
- emotions;
- atmosphere;
- social moments.

A restaurant website should make visitors imagine the experience before arriving.

---

# How Restaurant Experts Think

A restaurant website is not a digital menu.

It is a digital extension of the restaurant itself.

The website should communicate:

- personality;
- culinary identity;
- environment;
- quality.

A visitor should understand:

"Why should I eat here instead of somewhere else?"

---

# Business Objective

The main objective of a restaurant website is to increase:

- reservations;
- visits;
- customer confidence;
- brand recognition;
- repeat customers.

# Homepage Strategy

The homepage should immediately communicate:

- restaurant identity;
- cuisine;
- atmosphere;
- location.

The visitor should understand the experience within seconds.

---

# Homepage Recommended Flow

## 1. Hero Section

The hero should create immediate desire.

Include:

- strong photography or video;
- restaurant identity;
- clear value proposition;
- reservation action.

The goal is appetite and curiosity.

---

# 2. Restaurant Identity

Explain:

- philosophy;
- cuisine;
- inspiration;
- uniqueness.

Avoid generic descriptions.

---

# 3. Signature Experience

Highlight what makes the restaurant memorable.

Examples:

- signature dishes;
- chef philosophy;
- unique environment;
- cultural influences.

---

# 4. Food Showcase

Food presentation should create desire.

Use:

- high-quality photography;
- storytelling;
- selected dishes.

Avoid showing only random food images.

---

# Menu Strategy

The menu is one of the most important pages.

It is not just a list of dishes.

It communicates:

- quality;
- positioning;
- expectations.

---

# Menu Presentation Principles

A good digital menu should be:

- easy to read;
- visually appealing;
- updated;
- mobile-friendly.

---

# Menu Information

Consider including:

- dish names;
- descriptions;
- ingredients;
- dietary information;
- pricing.

---

# Menu Psychology

Descriptions should create interest.

Weak:

"Chicken with vegetables."

Better:

A description that communicates:

- preparation;
- ingredients;
- experience.

---

# Menu Photography

Use images strategically.

Avoid:

- photographing every dish poorly;
- inconsistent styles;
- excessive images.

Focus on:

- signature dishes;
- experiences;
- highlights.

---

# Reservation Experience

The reservation process should be simple.

The customer should easily find:

- availability;
- dates;
- times;
- contact options.

---

# Reservation CTA Strategy

Important actions should be visible.

Examples:

- Reserve a Table
- Book Your Experience
- Make a Reservation

Avoid unclear actions.

---

# Reservation Friction

Reduce:

- unnecessary forms;
- confusing steps;
- hidden contact information.

---

# Mobile Reservation Experience

Many customers search restaurants while outside.

Mobile should prioritize:

- quick loading;
- visible booking button;
- easy navigation;
- maps integration.

---

# Location Strategy

Location information should include:

- address;
- map;
- opening hours;
- parking information;
- nearby references.

---

# Opening Hours

Opening hours must be:

- visible;
- updated;
- easy to find.

Incorrect hours damage trust.

# Restaurant Industry Anti-Patterns

Common mistakes:

---

## Generic Website

Problem:

The restaurant feels like every other restaurant.

Solution:

Strengthen:

- identity;
- story;
- visuals.

---

## Poor Food Presentation

Problem:

Food does not look appealing.

Solution:

Improve:

- photography;
- composition;
- storytelling.

---

## Missing Menu Information

Problem:

Customers cannot decide.

Solution:

Provide:

- updated menu;
- prices when appropriate;
- clear descriptions.

---

## Difficult Reservation

Problem:

Customers abandon the process.

Solution:

Simplify booking.

---

## Weak Mobile Experience

Problem:

Customers cannot quickly find information.

Solution:

Optimize for mobile behavior.

---

## Outdated Information

Problem:

Wrong hours or menus reduce trust.

Solution:

Maintain accurate information.
