- Cafés

# Core Principle

Local business websites do not only provide information.

They help customers decide where to spend their money.

The main goals are:

- visibility;
- trust;
- convenience;
- contact.

A local business website should:

# Homepage Strategy

The homepage should immediately communicate:

- what the business offers;
- where it is located;
- why customers choose it.

Important elements:

- clear headline;
- services;
- location;
- reviews;
- contact CTA.

---

# Services & Products

Services should be easy to understand.

Include:

- descriptions;
- pricing when appropriate;
- benefits;
- availability.

Avoid forcing customers to ask basic questions.

---

# About Business

Local customers value authenticity.

---

# Location Experience

Location information should be obvious.

Include:

# Google Maps Integration

# Local Business Industry Anti-Patterns

Common mistakes include:

---

## Outdated Information

Problem:

Customers lose trust.

Solution:

Keep hours, services and contact information updated.

---

## No Real Photos

Problem:

The business feels anonymous.

Solution:

Show real products, people and location.

---

## Weak Mobile Experience

Problem:

Customers cannot easily contact the business.

Solution:

Optimize for quick actions.

---

## Ignoring Reviews

Problem:

Reputation suffers.

Solution:

Monitor and respond to feedback.

---

## Too Much Information

Problem:

Customers cannot find what they need.

Solution:

Prioritize clarity and simplicity.
