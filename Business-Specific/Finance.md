# Purpose

This document defines specialized principles for designing and developing digital experiences for financial organizations.

Supported businesses include:

- Banks
- Fintech companies
- Insurance companies
- Investment platforms
- Financial advisors
- Payment services
- Lending platforms

---

# Core Principle

Financial websites do not sell money.

They sell confidence.

Users are trusting organizations with:

- their money;
- their personal information;
- their financial future.

Every element should reinforce:

- security;
- transparency;
- reliability;
- expertise.

---

# Business Objectives

A financial website should:

- build trust;
- explain financial products;
- generate applications;
- support customers;
- simplify complex decisions.

---

# Financial Customer Psychology

Users usually ask:

- Is this company trustworthy?
- Is my money safe?
- Are the terms clear?
- Is this solution right for me?
- What are the risks?

The website should reduce uncertainty.

---

Financial trust is built through:

- protection;
- privacy;
- reliability;

---

## Transparency

Explain:

- fees;
- conditions;
- processes.

---

## Authority

Show:

- experience;
- certifications;
- partnerships;
- regulatory compliance.

---

## Simplicity

Financial topics are often complex.

The website should make decisions easier.

# Financial Product Communication

Financial products should never be explained only through features.

Explain:

- what it does;
- who it helps;
- why it matters;
- possible considerations.

---

# Financial Education

Many users avoid financial products because they feel complex.

Use:

- simple explanations;
- examples;
- calculators;
- educational content.

Education increases confidence.

---

# Final Principle

A great financial website makes complex decisions feel clear, secure and understandable.

7. Customer Portal
8. Support

# Homepage Strategy

The homepage should immediately communicate:

- what the company offers;
- who it serves;
- why users should trust it.

Important elements:

- clear value proposition;
- security signals;
- product highlights;
- strong CTA.

---

# Product Pages

Every financial product should have a dedicated page.

Examples:

- bank accounts;
- loans;
- insurance plans;
- investment products;
- payment solutions.

---

# Product Explanation Framework

Explain products through:

## Problem

What user need does it solve?

---

## Solution

How does the product help?

---

## Benefits

What value does the user receive?

---

## Conditions

What should users know before deciding?

Transparency builds trust.

# Dashboard Design

Financial dashboards should prioritize:

- clarity;
- hierarchy;
- important actions.

Avoid overwhelming users with unnecessary data.

---

# Data Visualization

Use visualizations to simplify information.

Examples:

- spending categories;
- investment performance;
- payment history;
- financial goals.

---

# Application Flow

Financial applications should be simple.

Reduce unnecessary steps.

Clearly communicate:

- progress;
- requirements;
- next actions.

---

# Forms & Data Collection

Financial forms require balance.

Collect necessary information while maintaining a simple experience.

Explain why sensitive information is required.

---

# Customer Support

Provide accessible support through:

- live chat;
- FAQ;
- help center;
- contact options.

Trust increases when help is easy to reach.

---

# Final Principle

Financial websites should guide users through decisions, not force them to understand everything alone.

# Security & Trust Experience

Security should not only exist technically.

It should also be communicated clearly.

Users need to feel protected.

- authentication systems;

- misleading statements.

# Financial Industry Anti-Patterns

Common mistakes include:

---

## Lack of Transparency

Problem:

Users cannot understand costs or conditions.

Solution:

Explain information clearly.

---

## Excessive Complexity

Problem:

Financial language creates confusion.

Solution:

Use simple explanations.

---

## Weak Trust Communication

Problem:

Users do not feel secure.

Solution:

Show expertise, security and credibility.

---

## Aggressive Sales Approach

Problem:

Pressure reduces confidence.

Solution:

Educate before converting.

---

## Poor Digital Experience

Problem:

Users struggle with basic actions.

Solution:

Create simple and efficient flows.
