# Travel Industry Anti-Patterns

Common mistakes include:

---

## Generic Destination Presentation

Problem:

The website looks like every competitor.

Solution:

Create unique stories and experiences.

---

## Hidden Information

Problem:

Travelers cannot understand what they are buying.

Solution:

Show clear itineraries, prices and inclusions.

---

## Weak Visual Experience

Problem:

The destination does not create desire.

Solution:

Use strong photography and storytelling.

---

## Complicated Booking

Problem:

Visitors abandon before purchase.

Solution:

Simplify the reservation process.

---

## Lack of Trust

Problem:

Customers hesitate with expensive trips.

Solution:

Show reviews, guarantees and real experiences.
