# Purpose

Supported businesses include:

- Gyms
- Fitness studios
- Personal trainers
- Online coaching programs
- Wellness centers

# Core Principle

Fitness websites do not only sell workouts.

- results;
- motivation;

- accountability.

# Fitness Industry Anti-Patterns

Common mistakes include:

---

## Unrealistic Transformation Claims

Problem:

Creates distrust.

Solution:

Show realistic progress and authentic experiences.

---

## Generic Motivation

Problem:

The brand feels identical to competitors.

Solution:

Communicate a unique approach.

---

## Hidden Membership Information

Problem:

Creates hesitation.

Solution:

Explain plans clearly.

---

## Poor Community Communication

Problem:

The business feels transactional.

Solution:

Show people, culture and belonging.

---

## Complex Booking

Problem:

Users abandon the process.

Solution:

Make scheduling simple.
