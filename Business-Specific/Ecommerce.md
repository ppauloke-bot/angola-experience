# Purpose

This document defines specialized principles for designing and developing high-converting digital experiences for ecommerce businesses.

Supported businesses include:

- Online stores
- Fashion brands
- Consumer products
- Luxury brands
- Direct-to-consumer brands
- Marketplace businesses
- Subscription products

---

# Core Principle

Ecommerce websites do not sell products.

They sell confidence in a purchase decision.

Customers need to believe:

- the product solves their need;
- the quality is worth the price;
- the brand is trustworthy;
- the purchase process is safe.

---

# Business Objectives

An ecommerce website should:

- generate sales;
- reduce purchase hesitation;
- increase average order value;
- build customer loyalty;
- create repeat purchases.

---

# Ecommerce Customer Psychology

Customers usually ask:

- Is this product right for me?
- Is the quality good?
- Can I trust this brand?
- Is the price justified?
- What happens if I do not like it?

The website should answer these questions before checkout.

# Ecommerce Industry Anti-Patterns

Common mistakes include:

---

## Weak Product Presentation

Problem:

Customers cannot understand the product.

Solution:

Improve images, descriptions and demonstrations.

---

## Lack of Trust

Problem:

Visitors hesitate to buy.

Solution:

Show reviews, guarantees and transparency.

---

## Complicated Checkout

Problem:

Customers abandon purchases.

Solution:

Reduce unnecessary steps.

---

## Poor Mobile Experience

Problem:

Users struggle to shop.

Solution:

Design mobile-first.

---

## No Retention Strategy

Problem:

The business depends only on new customers.

Solution:

Build loyalty and relationships.
