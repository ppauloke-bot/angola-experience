# Copywriting

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines principles for creating clear, persuasive and human-centered website copy.

Copywriting should communicate value, create connection and guide users toward meaningful actions.

Words are part of the user experience.

---

# Core Principle

Do not write to fill space.

Write to communicate value.

Every sentence should have a purpose.

---

# Copywriting Mindset

Before writing, understand:

- Who is the audience?
- What problem exists?
- What does the brand offer?
- Why should users care?
- What action should happen next?

Good copy starts with understanding.

---

# Avoid Generic Copy

Avoid phrases such as:

- "best solutions";
- "innovative experiences";
- "quality and excellence";
- "we are committed to excellence";
- "the future of your business".

These phrases usually communicate nothing.

---

# Specificity Creates Trust

Prefer specific communication.

Weak:

"Luxury experience."

Strong:

"A private oceanfront retreat designed for complete relaxation."

Specific details create credibility.

---

# Brand Voice

Every brand should have a recognizable voice.

Consider:

- personality;
- audience;
- industry;
- positioning.

Examples:

Luxury brand:

- confident;
- refined;
- minimal.

Local business:

- human;
- trustworthy;
- approachable.

Technology brand:

- precise;
- innovative;
- clear.

---

# User-Centered Writing

Focus on the user's perspective.

Prefer:

"You can..."

"You will experience..."

"Designed for..."

Avoid making everything about the company.

---

# Headlines

A strong headline should:

- capture attention;
- communicate value;
- create curiosity.

Avoid:

- empty statements;
- exaggerated claims;
- unclear messages.

---

# Hero Copy Principles

The hero section should quickly answer:

- What is this?
- Why does it matter?
- What should I do next?

A strong hero usually contains:

- clear headline;
- supporting message;
- relevant action.

---

# Call To Action (CTA)

Every CTA should be clear.

Good examples:

- Book your stay
- Schedule a consultation
- Explore the collection
- Request availability

Avoid vague CTAs:

- Learn more;
- Click here;
- Submit.

---

# Storytelling

Strong websites create a journey.

Structure information around:

1. User problem
2. Desired outcome
3. Solution
4. Proof
5. Action

---

# Trust Building

Trust can come from:

- testimonials;
- experience;
- certifications;
- results;
- real details;
- transparency.

Do not invent proof.

---

# Luxury Copy Principles

Luxury communication often uses:

- confidence;
- simplicity;
- restraint;
- emotion.

Avoid:

- excessive adjectives;
- desperation;
- aggressive selling.

Premium brands create desire, not pressure.

---

# Conversion Principles

Good copy helps users make decisions.

Consider:

- clarity;
- objections;
- trust;
- motivation.

Do not manipulate users.

---

# SEO and Copy

Write naturally.

Do not sacrifice readability for keywords.

Search optimization should support useful content.

---

# AI Writing Rules

Never generate generic marketing language.

Before writing ask:

- Does this sound like this specific brand?
- Could another company use the same sentence?
- Does this create real value?

If yes, improve it.

---

# Copy Review Checklist

Before delivery:

□ Is the message clear?

□ Does it communicate real value?

□ Does it sound human?

□ Does it match the brand?

□ Does it avoid clichés?

□ Does it guide the user?

□ Are claims supported?

---

# Final Principle

Great copy does not convince people through more words.

It creates understanding.

The goal is not to sound impressive.

The goal is to make the user understand why it matters.
