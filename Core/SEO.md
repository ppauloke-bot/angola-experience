# SEO

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines SEO principles for creating websites that are discoverable, understandable and valuable for users and search engines.

SEO is not a separate task.

SEO is part of building a high-quality digital experience.

---

# Core Principle

The goal of SEO is not to manipulate search engines.

The goal is to create the best possible answer and experience for users.

---

# SEO Mindset

Before optimizing, understand:

- Who is searching?
- What are they trying to achieve?
- What information do they need?
- What action should they take?

Good SEO starts with user intent.

---

# Search Intent

Every page should have a clear purpose.

Consider:

## Informational Intent

Users want knowledge.

Examples:

- guides;
- explanations;
- articles.

---

## Commercial Intent

Users are comparing options.

Examples:

- services;
- solutions;
- comparisons.

---

## Transactional Intent

Users are ready to act.

Examples:

- bookings;
- purchases;
- contact requests.

---

# Technical SEO

Consider:

- page structure;
- loading speed;
- mobile performance;
- crawlability;
- clean URLs.

Technical quality supports visibility.

---

# Semantic Structure

Use meaningful HTML.

Prioritize:

- headings;
- sections;
- navigation;
- structured content.

Search engines should understand the page.

---

# Metadata

Every important page should have:

- unique title;
- useful description;
- appropriate keywords.

Avoid:

- duplicate metadata;
- keyword stuffing;
- misleading descriptions.

---

# Content Quality

Content should be:

- useful;
- specific;
- accurate;
- easy to understand.

Avoid:

- generic AI-generated text;
- unnecessary length;
- repetition.

---

# Keyword Strategy

Use keywords naturally.

Do not force keywords into content.

Focus on:

- user language;
- search intent;
- relevant topics.

---

# Local SEO

Important for businesses with physical locations.

Consider:

- location information;
- services;
- contact details;
- business information consistency.

Examples:

- hotels;
- restaurants;
- clinics;
- local services.

---

# Structured Data

Use structured data when appropriate.

Examples:

- organization;
- local business;
- products;
- events;
- reviews.

Structured data helps search engines understand information.

---

# Images SEO

Optimize images through:

- descriptive filenames;
- appropriate alt text;
- compression;
- correct formats.

---

# Internal Linking

Create logical connections between pages.

Good internal linking helps:

- users navigate;
- search engines understand relationships.

---

# SEO and Performance

SEO depends on user experience.

Consider:

- speed;
- mobile usability;
- accessibility;
- stability.

A poor experience damages visibility.

---

# AI Content Rules

AI can help create content.

However:

Always review for:

- accuracy;
- originality;
- brand alignment;
- usefulness.

Do not publish generic AI content without refinement.

---

# SEO Review Checklist

Before delivery:

□ Pages have clear purposes

□ Titles and descriptions are optimized

□ Content matches user intent

□ Structure is semantic

□ Images are optimized

□ Performance is considered

□ Mobile experience is strong

□ Local SEO is applied when relevant

□ No keyword manipulation exists

---

# Final Principle

SEO is not about getting attention from search engines.

It is about creating something valuable enough to deserve attention.
